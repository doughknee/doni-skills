# Agency / Studio

**Slug:** `agency`
**What it is:** A creative or dev studio selling its taste: showcase, manifesto, services, team, contact.
**Default aesthetic (brief is silent):** `motion-first`

## Allowed aesthetics
- motion-first
- swiss
- neo-brutalism
- editorial
- collage
- maximalism
- grunge
- dark-saas
- minimalism
- luxury
- brutalism (anti-design studios)

## Banned aesthetics
- enterprise
- material
- alegria
- flat
- playful
- scandinavian

## Must-have sections
- Nav: wordmark and 2 to 4 links
- Statement hero (type-led, often one giant line)
- Selected work (3 to 6 projects, large)
- Services or capabilities list
- Manifesto or approach
- Team
- Contact with a real email

## Layout skeleton
- Type at display scale: hero headline 64 to 120px
- Work shown large: one project per viewport or 2-up
- Horizontal scroll and sticky sections are allowed
- Max-width can exceed 1200px; some aesthetics here go edge to edge

## Component list
- Wordmark nav
- Statement hero
- Project tile (full-bleed image, hover reveals title)
- Service list with numbers
- Team card
- Marquee

## Notes
- This is the one context where the loud four are reasonable defaults
- Load the `frontend-design` skill after the recipe; agency sites are judged on polish
