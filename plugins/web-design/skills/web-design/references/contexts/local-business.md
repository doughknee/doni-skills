# Local Business

**Slug:** `local-business`
**What it is:** A physical place or service area: restaurant, shop, clinic, contractor, gym. Visitors want hours, location, services, and a phone number.
**Default aesthetic (brief is silent):** `scandinavian`

## Allowed aesthetics
- scandinavian
- enterprise (professional services)
- playful (family and kids)
- photo-led (food, venues)
- hand-drawn (cafes, makers)
- flat
- organic (wellness, landscaping)
- grunge (bars, tattoo, wrestling)
- pixel (arcades, game bars)
- art-deco (upscale hospitality)

## Banned aesthetics
- Tech/Dark family
- motion-first
- brutalism
- vaporwave
- cyberpunk
- maximalism

## Must-have sections
- Nav with phone number visible
- Hero with one sentence of what and where, CTA (call, book, directions)
- Services or menu
- About / story
- Hours and location with embedded map
- Reviews
- Contact form
- Footer with address and hours again

## Layout skeleton
- Max-width 1080px
- Phone number in the nav on every page, tap-to-call on mobile
- Hours block uses a two-column table
- Map embed full-width in its section

## Component list
- Nav with phone CTA
- Hero
- Service card
- Hours table
- Map embed
- Review card
- Contact form

## Notes
- Mobile is the primary viewport; most visits are from a phone looking for hours
- Sticky bottom call bar on mobile is allowed in every aesthetic here
